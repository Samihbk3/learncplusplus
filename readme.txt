Your English

Your message is understandable, but there are several spelling and phrasing improvements.

You wrote:

“Give me excersices for c++ I reached to loops.”

More natural:

“Give me some C++ exercises. I’ve reached loops.”

Or:

“Give me some C++ exercises. I’ve learned up to loops.”

“Exercises” is spelled with an i, not “excersices.”

Also, “I reached to loops” is not natural English. We don’t normally use “reach to” when talking about progress in a course. “I’ve reached loops” or “I’ve learned up to loops” is better.

And:

“I will perform on vs code”

would be more naturally:

“I’ll do them in VS Code.”

“Do exercises” is the normal expression in American English.
